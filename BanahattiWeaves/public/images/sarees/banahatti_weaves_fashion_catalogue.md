## 4. Brand Photography Guidelines for Banahatti Weaves

To ensure consistent product imagery across all products on the Banahatti Weaves online store, every photoshoot adheres strictly to these parameters:

| Aesthetic Parameter | Catalogue Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Model** | Graceful Indian female model, natural makeup, subtle traditional golden jewelry |
| **Lighting** | Soft diffused natural studio key light with fill for organic fabric texture rendering |
| **Background** | Warm neutral cream/beige boutique backdrop, minimal shadows, non-distracting |
| **Drape Style** | Classic Indian Nivi drape, 3/4 or full-body pose displaying pallu, body checks, and border |
| **Purity** | 100% preservation of weave pattern, border width, colors, and temple (*toperi*) motifs |
| **Quality** | High resolution e-commerce fashion catalogue standards, crisp fabric detail, no text/watermarks |
